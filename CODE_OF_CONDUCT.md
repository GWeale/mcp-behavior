# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Treat other contributors with respect. Discuss the work directly, assume mistakes are fixable, and give people room to ask basic questions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this standard. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as privately as the circumstances allow.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
